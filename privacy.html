Privacy Policy

App: Diego Photo Pipeline · Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Diego Photo Pipeline application ("the App") handles information. The App is a private, personal automation tool used by its owner to publish their own photographs to their own Instagram account. It is not offered to or used by the general public.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated by an individual ("the Operator") for personal use. Contact: round_scoop0o@icloud.com.

2. What the App accesses

The App uses the Instagram Graph API solely to publish content to the Operator's own Instagram professional account. Specifically it accesses:

  • The Operator's own image files, which the Operator selects for posting;
  • Caption text generated for, or written by, the Operator;
  • A long-lived access token that authorizes posting to the Operator's own account.

3. What the App does not do

  • It does not collect, request, or process personal data from any other person or from followers of the account.
  • It does not sell, share, rent, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling, or analytics about individuals.
  • It does not access direct messages, follower lists, or other users' private information.

4. Data storage and retention

Image files, captions, and the access token are stored locally on the Operator's own computer. Images are temporarily hosted in a private staging location only for the brief moment required to transfer them to Instagram, and are not retained for any other purpose. No data is stored on third-party servers beyond Instagram itself.

5. Third-party services

The App interacts with Meta's Instagram Graph API to publish content. Use of Instagram is governed by Meta's own Privacy Policy.

6. Data deletion

Because the App processes only the Operator's own content on the Operator's own account, the Operator may delete any published content directly within Instagram, and may revoke the App's access at any time from Instagram's account settings. To request deletion of any data held by the App, contact the Operator at the email above.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process any data relating to children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to round_scoop0o@icloud.com.
